EXPERIMENT 02 · SOLAR POWER

When the light
begins to fade.

Solar-powered explorers need sunlight. Move the dust slider to see a simple model of how sunlight reaching a solar panel can drop as atmospheric dust increases.

SIMULATED LIGHT REACHING PANEL75%

This is a simplified teaching model, not real atmospheric measurements or mission power data. Opportunity last contacted Earth during a major dust storm; InSight also experienced declining solar power as dust accumulated on its panels.

RESEARCH DESK / SOURCE NOTES

Grounded in real exploration.

Every mission fact is based on a linked NASA source. Mission photographs come from NASA image archives. First-person voices, atlas marker locations and learning demonstrations are creative educational interpretations.

Research, not fiction.
Storytelling, not misinformation.

We keep a clear line between historical records and imagined machine voices. Every source opens directly to the agency’s original mission page or archive.
